# Privacy Policy — Easy Logic Simulator

**Last updated: October 19, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Easy Logic Simulator ("the app", "we") handles your information. Easy Logic Simulator is a digital logic circuit drawing and simulation application published by Mordia Studio.

## Summary

Easy Logic Simulator **does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data**. The app works entirely offline on your device. We have no servers, no user accounts, and no analytics.

## Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect:

- your name, email address, or any contact information;
- your location;
- usage or analytics data;
- advertising identifiers;
- the contents of your circuit designs or any files you create.

We do not use cookies, trackers, or third-party analytics or advertising services.

## Your files

Circuits and projects you create (including `.els` project files) are saved only on your own device, in locations you choose or in the app's local storage. When you open a project file from another app, it is read locally on your device. If you use the app's share or export feature, files are shared only with the apps and recipients you explicitly select. We never receive a copy of your files.

## In-app purchases

Easy Logic Simulator offers an optional one-time in-app purchase (premium upgrade, which removes the free limit on subcircuits) processed entirely by the app store on your platform (Google Play, Apple App Store, or Microsoft Store). Payment and billing information is handled by the store operator under its own privacy policy; we never see or store your payment details. The app queries the store only to display the product's price and to check whether you own the purchase, and this check involves no personal data being sent to us.

## Internet access

The app requests internet access solely to communicate with the platform's app store for the purchase and license verification described above. No other network communication takes place.

## Children's privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13. It is suitable for general audiences.

## Data security

Because all of your data stays on your device, its security is governed by your device's own protections. We recommend keeping your operating system up to date.

## Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete. Your circuit files are fully under your control and can be deleted from your device at any time. Purchases can be managed or refunded through your app store account.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change (for example, if a future version adds online features), this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised before those changes take effect.
